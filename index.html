YouTube timing is approximate. The counter and clicks follow an interpolated copy of the player's clock. It runs smoothly, but what you hear can be a few tens of ms off from it (audio output delay inside the YouTube player), and it can slip for a moment after a seek, a speed change or buffering. If the clicks sound early or late, move click offset. YouTube only offers a few speeds (0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.25 here). During an ad the counter waits for the music. If a whole song is off, fix it with YouTube offset below. For exact timing, use the local mp3. After clicking inside the video, click the page once so the keyboard shortcuts work again.
